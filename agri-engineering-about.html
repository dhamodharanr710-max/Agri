About the Department

Our department is dedicated to developing innovative engineering solutions for agricultural challenges. From farm mechanization to food processing, irrigation systems to renewable energy in agriculture — we cover it all.

We collaborate with leading agricultural institutes, industry partners, and farming communities to ensure our research has real-world impact, translating laboratory findings into tools that farmers can actually use.
